Chicago DC Night Heat
183 listed data centers (113 mapped) vs matched controls · ECOSTRESS LST

We mapped Chicago-area data centers from DataCenterMap, then compared nighttime land-surface temperature in buffers around each site with matched non–data-center controls. Observational comparison, not a causal claim. Mean Δ at 500 m ≈ +2.4 °C (July 2025 sample).

Study design

Question: are neighborhoods around Chicago data centers hotter at night than comparable nearby areas?

  1. Why these sites: 183 Chicago-area data centers from DataCenterMap; 113 geocoded points are the study locations (infrastructure + environmental comparison).
  2. Nighttime LST background: one ECOSTRESS ISS pass (~70 m), mosaicked from same-timestamp tiles and clipped to the cluster. The pipeline processed 22 nights; this image is the max-coverage pass (2025-07-31). Not a live feed.
  3. Warmer than control (Δ°C): each data-center buffer vs matched non–data-center controls at the same timestamps. Positive means the site ran warmer. Default 500 m.
  4. Click a site or polygon: mean Δ°C, observation count, and an observational caveat.
  5. Heat context score: optional; off by default. Opening-date fields appear in popups only when a verified go-live exists.
